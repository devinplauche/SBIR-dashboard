Fit scores are a keyword heuristic over topic text, tuned to surface software-only work a small team can build without a lab, a fab, a clearance, or a university partner. They rank a shortlist for a human to read — they do not judge merit, and a low score is not a verdict.

Deadlines and award ceilings vary by component. SBIR.gov and DSIP mirror agency documents and are not authoritative — confirm against the agency's own solicitation before relying on anything here.
